# Full-Moon observation

Place the local `earth-clouds.png` and `moon.jpg` textures in `src/SpaceTangent.Godot/.local-data/surface` or set `SPACETANGENT_SURFACE_TEXTURES` to their directory. The previous project's preserved test assets have SHA-256 values `bf7eb4efd942224e7cc028388b96ae250eb8f29a679c0e4103123b0ea8136dff` and `6f684f9125ed90f9f10fc0c4d769b3cc75d7fd68c639e9094a24cee1a0e02374` respectively. They remain external to the repository.

**Full Moon** opens the Moscow surface view at JD 2460600.5008 TDB. It uses the previous regression's heading of 237°, pitch of 28°, 10° vertical field and 4× apparent lunar size. The shader samples a spherical cloud shell, applies sunlight during the day and adds reflected lunar light on the night side when the Moon is above the local horizon. Lunar flux follows phase, distance and a bounded Earth-shadow estimate. The cloud exposure is a display setting. The surface view uses a mean sidereal Earth approximation.

The spherical Earth atmosphere extends to 100 km. It integrates single scattering along the observer ray, with separate exponential profiles for Rayleigh scattering and aerosols. A Chapman column approximates extinction along the solar ray. The Earth's shadow and the Sun's angular diameter determine the illumination of each sample. The Moon uses the full atmospheric transmission. The cloud shell uses the accumulated transmission and scattering between the observer and the cloud. This model treats absorption with the Rayleigh height profile. The display exposure is fixed. Multiple scattering is outside this implementation.

[Bucholtz, Rayleigh scattering calculations](https://opg.optica.org/ao/abstract.cfm?uri=ao-34-15-2765) · [Bruneton and Neyret, atmospheric light transport](https://inria.hal.science/inria-00288758)

To reproduce the GPU check, set `SPACETANGENT_MOON_OUTPUT` to an absolute output directory and launch `res://tests/MoonLightingProbe.tscn` with rendering enabled. The probe captures the cloud controls at the same full-Moon and daytime epochs, plus unobscured daytime, twilight and near-horizon sky images. It compares their RGB samples against double-precision numerical quadrature along both light paths and measures the cloud brightness change at night.
